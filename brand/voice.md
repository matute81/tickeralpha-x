# Voice

Write like a desk briefing, not a newsletter, not a product ad, not a Telegram signal group.

## Sound like this

- Calm, specific, slightly dry.
- Lead with a fact, a date, or a number. Then the read.
- Short paragraphs. Bullets when there are several prints or names.
- One idea per post. The recap is the exception: it is the log of the day.
- Observation, not a call. “Yields fell and duration-sensitive names caught a bid” is fine. “Buy QQQ here” is not.

## Do not sound like this

- Hype: moon, crush, insane, must-see, breaking (unless something actually broke).
- Fake urgency: “last chance,” “don’t miss this.”
- Motivational trader: grind, discipline, “trust the process.”
- Hashtag soup. Default is zero hashtags.
- “Not financial advice” on every post. Use one short line only if the copy could be read as a recommendation.
- Product pitch. Do not name heatmaps, Congress, insiders, or tickeralpha.ai unless the sentence is weaker without it. In v1, leave the product out.

## Words to prefer

close, print, consensus, prior, breadth, duration, mega-cap, equal-weight, overnight, after the bell, faded, held, led, lagged

## Words to avoid

guaranteed, easy money, moon, rocket, secret, unlock, crush the market, to the moon, FOMO, alpha leak (as a tease)

## Cross-slot rule

Do not paste the same paragraph into two posts. The recap lists what happened. The story of the day is the one narrative. Movers own the tickers. Cross-asset owns yields, dollar, oil, gold, vol.

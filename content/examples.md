## Story of the day

Today was not a “stocks were mixed” day. It was a duration day.

The S&P barely moved. The Nasdaq did the work on the downside, and it did it while the 10-year was up 6bp. That is the whole story: when yields back up, the index that is a long-duration tech book is the one that pays.

If you only watched the S&P, you missed it. If you watched yields first, the Nasdaq close was the obvious second screen.

## How to read the print

CPI tomorrow, 08:30 ET.

Headline CPI is the all-items index. Core CPI strips food and energy. Markets usually trade core, then the supercore / services color if it is in the release text. A “beat” means the print came in hotter than consensus (higher inflation). A “miss” means cooler.

What to map it to: yields first, then Nasdaq vs Dow. Do not decide the whole Fed path off one decimal if claims and PCE have been telling a different story. Tomorrow’s job is: does this number confirm the last two prints, or contradict them?

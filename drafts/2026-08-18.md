## Aug. 18 session recap

**Status:** ready
**Suggested window:** Stream 2 — Price movements & stock analysis

### Body

U.S. equity benchmarks finished lower after a session widely described in headlines as chip and AI-related weakness alongside elevated long-term bond yields.
The Nasdaq Composite led declines among the large caps, while the Dow held up relatively better.

The S&P 500 closed at 7,693.26, down 51.80 points (−0.7%).
The Nasdaq Composite settled at 26,289.71 (−1.3%).
The Dow Jones Industrial Average ended at 53,343.40 (−0.2%).
The Russell 2000 finished at 3,017.89 (−1.3%).

Early in the day, July housing data hit the wire at 12:30 ET.
Building permits came in at 1.443M (estimate 1.37M), with permits up 5.0% month over month (estimate +1.2%).
Housing starts were 1.239M (estimate 1.35M), with starts down 12.4% month over month (estimate −4.7%).
Later, the Atlanta Fed GDPNow reading for Q3 was 4.0% (estimate and previous 4.3%).

Sector averages on Nasdaq showed Utilities down about 3.1% and Real Estate down about 1.6%, while Financial Services averaged about −1.2%.
Healthcare and Industrials were among the relative standouts, each up about 0.5%.
Technology averaged about −0.6%.

Semiconductor and large tech-linked names carried much of the index weight.
NVIDIA closed at 219.74 (−2.3%).
Broadcom finished at 380.00 (−3.2%).
ASML ended at 1,802.98 (−4.3%).
Oracle closed at 142.67 (−2.7%).
Intel, among the most active names in the session data, closed at 96.69 (−6.6%).

Healthcare megacaps bucked the broader risk-off tone in places.
Eli Lilly closed at 1,226.67 (+3.7%).
AbbVie finished at 258.92 (+3.4%).
Payment networks also firmed: Mastercard at 574.31 (+2.1%) and Visa at 364.25 (+1.5%).
Salesforce closed at 196.14 (+2.7%); Netflix at 77.77 (+2.3%).

The earnings calendar for the session included Home Depot, which reported EPS of 4.92 vs 4.73 estimated and revenue of about 47.86B vs about 47.24B estimated; the stock closed at 337.49 (−0.1%).
Keysight beat on EPS (3.07 vs 2.48) and revenue but closed at 341.30 (−5.5%).
Toll Brothers and Jack Henry also reported beats on EPS; Toll closed −1.8% and Jack Henry +2.2%.
Mercury Systems beat revenue but missed slightly on EPS (0.37 vs 0.38) and closed −7.4%.

Cross-asset readings in the injected close data showed Brent crude at 91.71 (+0.8%) and the United States Oil Fund at 130.66 (+0.3%).
Gold futures were at 4,395.20 (−0.6%).
The dollar proxy (UUP) was 28.14 (+0.1%).
Bitcoin was 64,392.36 (−0.1%).
The VIX rose to 15.84 (+4.3%).

Treasury yield levels in the dataset (as of 2026-08-14) showed the 10-year at 4.7% and the 30-year at 5.2%, with a 2s10s spread of 0.51 percentage points — context that matched the day’s headline focus on long-term borrowing costs.

Overseas, the Nikkei 225 was down 2.5% at 65,788.99 in the session’s global index set.
The FTSE 100 was roughly flat (+0.1%).
The Hang Seng edged up 0.1%.

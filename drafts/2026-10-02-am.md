## September payrolls add 29K vs 90K expected

**Status:** ready
**Suggested window:** Stream 1 — Economy, politics & news

**Short Summary:** The September jobs report showed far fewer new jobs than economists expected, unemployment ticked up to 4.2%, and major US stock indexes closed higher on the session.

### Post Body

US employers added 29,000 jobs in September.

Economists had expected about 90,000; August was revised context at 133,000.

The unemployment rate — the share of people in the labor force without work — came in at 4.2%, above the 4.1% forecast and up from 4.1% in August.

Private payrolls rose 46,000 vs an 85,000 estimate.

## Hourly pay up 0.1% MoM, 3.0% YoY

**Status:** ready
**Suggested window:** Stream 1 — Economy, politics & news

**Short Summary:** Average hourly earnings rose less than expected in September on both a month-over-month and year-over-year basis, alongside a drop in government payrolls.

### Post Body

Average hourly earnings — what workers are paid per hour on average — rose 0.1% from August to September.

The consensus had been 0.3% month over month; August was 0.3%.

Year over year, pay was up 3.0% vs a 3.2% estimate and 3.1% prior.

Government payrolls fell by 17,000 jobs; the estimate had been +15,000, with +44,000 in the prior month.

The broader underemployment rate (U-6), which includes discouraged workers and part-timers who want full-time work, was 7.6% vs a 7.7% forecast.

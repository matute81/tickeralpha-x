## October wholesale inflation up 0.3%

**Status:** ready
**Suggested window:** Stream 1 — Economy, politics & news

**Short Summary:** U.S. producer prices rose 0.3% month over month in October, a tenth below the 0.4% economists expected and well under September’s 1.4% pace.

### Post Body

October’s Producer Price Index (wholesale inflation for goods and services businesses buy) came in at +0.3% month over month.

Economists had expected +0.4%; September was +1.4%.

Year over year, producer prices are up 6.5% vs a 6.6% estimate (September: 6.3%).

Core PPI, which strips volatile food and energy, matched forecasts at +0.3% MoM and +5.0% YoY.

“PPI ex food, energy and trade” — a narrower core measure — rose +0.5% MoM vs a +0.3% estimate.

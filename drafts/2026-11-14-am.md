## Chicago Fed: missing on inflation

**Status:** ready
**Suggested window:** Stream 1 — Economy, politics & news

**Short Summary:** Chicago Fed President Austan Goolsbee said the Fed is not hitting its inflation goal — the “price stability” half of its two-part mandate — and pointed to demand pressure tied to large AI data-center construction.

### Post Body

Chicago Fed President Goolsbee: the Fed is “failing on the inflation side of the dual mandate” — the dual mandate is maximum employment plus stable prices (inflation near target).

He said inflation has run above target for three or four months and is moving the wrong way in categories that are hard to reverse quickly.

He linked part of the pressure to AI data-center build-out, calling it a historically large stimulus that can push demand above what the economy can smoothly absorb (“overheating”).

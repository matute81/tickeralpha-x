**Status:** ready
**Suggested window:** Stream 1 — Economy, politics & news

**Short Summary:** Moody's says the US economy grew more than expected and now sees full-year growth at 2.3%, with a slower path ahead tied to higher borrowing costs, energy prices, and a cooling cycle in AI-related spending.

### Post Body

Moody's expects US full-year economic growth of 2.3% for 2026 — above what many had been assuming after recent data.

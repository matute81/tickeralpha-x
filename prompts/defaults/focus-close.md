Lead with the move, not the ticker. A megacap moving 0.5% is more market
impact than a $1.6B company moving 44%. Priority: big named tickers with
real moves and a sourced cause (headline, earnings, guidance, insider
activity), then industry themes where multiple names moved together, then
technical structure (crossed 50d/200d, broke 52-week level, drawdown from
all-time high). Every percentage, price, and ticker must come from the
injected EOD or profile data.

Then write Idea 4: a previous-session recap. Summarize everything the
injected data shows for that US cash session — indexes, the print if one
printed, sectors, named movers, earnings with actuals, and the cross-asset
tape (yields, dollar, gold, crude, VIX). Use 10 to 12 points, each as its
own short paragraph (not a bullet list). Long-form. Cover the day in
order of what moved the tape, not every ticker. Idea 4 may repeat names
from ideas 1–3 because it is the full log. No forecast. No engagement
question required on Idea 4.
